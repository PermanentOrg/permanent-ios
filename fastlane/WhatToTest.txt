Version 1.17.1 fixes a crash at launch. When the server, or something between the app and the server, answered with a web page instead of data, the app closed on its own. It now shows the usual "Server error has occured. Please try again." alert and keeps running. Two views were also tidied for iOS 18: the confirmation dialog and the upload progress bars.

This is a production build. It uses your real account, your real files and real payments.

Please focus on:

1. Launch. Open the app, sign in if asked, and wait for Private Files. The list must load. Close the app from the app switcher and open it again a few times: it must come back to Private Files every time, with no crash and no sign-out.

2. Move from the list. Hold a file for a moment, tap Move, pick a folder, tap Move Here. A small circle at the bottom must spin, then show a check mark. The file must be in that folder.

3. Move from the file details. Open a file, tap the three-dot button, tap File information, tap Move, pick a folder. The file must move, with no error alert.

4. Copy. Hold a file for a moment, tap Copy, pick a folder, tap Paste Here. The circle must spin, then show the check mark. A copy must be in that folder.

5. Share from Photos. In the Photos app, open a picture, tap Share, tap Permanent, tap Upload. The picture must upload and show in Private Files.

6. iOS 18. On a phone with iOS 18, upload a few photos and watch the progress bars. Then delete a file and confirm in the dialog. Both must work with no crash.

7. Everything else. A quick pass over folders, shares, publishing and uploads is welcome. Nothing else changed in this build, which ships on top of 1.17.0.

If the service is ever down while you test, the app must show "Server error has occured. Please try again." and stay open. Please report any time the app closes on its own.

Known, please do not file:
- The spelling "occured" in the server error alert is the current text and is not part of this fix.
